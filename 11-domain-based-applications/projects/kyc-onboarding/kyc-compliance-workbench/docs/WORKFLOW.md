# KYC lifecycle
1. Receive application and customer/product details.
2. Collect identity and address evidence.
3. Review authenticity, validity, completeness, and consistency.
4. Invoke approved identity verification provider.
5. Perform policy-required sanctions, PEP, adverse-media and internal watchlist screening.
6. Assess customer risk using approved policy and evidence.
7. Perform enhanced due diligence and additional approvals where applicable.
8. Approve, reject, or request more information.
9. Perform ongoing monitoring and periodic refresh.
Requirements vary by jurisdiction, customer type, product, and institutional policy. This demo is not regulatory advice.
