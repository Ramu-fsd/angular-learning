# Domain workflows

## Policy servicing
1. Search policy portfolio.
2. Open policy record and verify policyholder, product, effective dates and coverage.
3. Confirm policy status through the policy service.
4. Route endorsements, renewals and cancellations through authorized backend workflows.

## Claims lifecycle
1. First notice of loss (FNOL) is captured.
2. Claim is linked to a policy and coverage is verified server-side.
3. Triage determines priority and assigns an adjuster.
4. Evidence is collected and validated.
5. Investigation and assessment determine liability and reserve.
6. Authorized approver records settlement decision.
7. Payment service executes approved disbursement.
8. Claim closure and audit evidence are retained.

The demo only illustrates queue navigation, evidence receipt indicators, assignment and status updates. It does not implement real claim adjudication.
