# Domain workflows

## Incident lifecycle

1. Monitoring or a service desk user creates an incident.
2. Operations triages impact and urgency.
3. Priority and ownership are assigned.
4. Analysts investigate and add handover notes.
5. Work moves through New, In Progress, Pending, and Resolved.
6. Resolution evidence is retained in the activity history.

## Service request lifecycle

1. A business team submits a request with requester and department details.
2. Operations validates completeness and prioritizes the request.
3. An analyst is assigned.
4. Fulfilment steps are performed and recorded.
5. The request is resolved and communicated to the requester.

## SLA handling

This portfolio demo marks items due within the next 24 hours as at risk. A production implementation must calculate SLA deadlines on the server, account for business calendars and pauses, and persist breach history.
