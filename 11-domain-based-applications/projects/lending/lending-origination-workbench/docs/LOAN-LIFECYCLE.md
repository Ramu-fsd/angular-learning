# Loan origination lifecycle

1. Application capture: applicant, requested amount, term, and product.
2. Identity and KYC: verify applicant identity through approved services.
3. Document collection: income, employment, bank statements, and supporting evidence.
4. Credit assessment: retrieve bureau data where permitted and applicable.
5. Affordability assessment: evaluate income, expenses, existing obligations, and policy limits.
6. Underwriting: assess risk, exceptions, collateral, and required approvals.
7. Decision: approve, decline, or request additional information.
8. Offer and acceptance: present terms and capture customer acceptance.
9. Disbursement: execute controlled funding workflow.
10. Servicing: repayment schedule, collections, changes, and closure.

Actual steps and controls vary by jurisdiction, lending product, and institutional policy.
